newspaper front page.

## The day's stories

- **COP31, Antalya** — climate talks run into a second day of overtime;
  the final draft omits any mention of "fossil fuels" or a phase-out of
  oil, coal and gas, and backs carbon capture instead.
- **The Gulf** — Iran strikes an industrial site in Bahrain; Bahrain
  blames Tehran as tankers are hit in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Mexico** — cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, "El Chapo Isidro",
  captured in a Sinaloa raid; 11 killed in the violence that followed.
- **Markets** — US stocks slide into correction as AI shares sell off;
  the S&P 500 falls 3.7%.
- **Indonesia** — a fire at a sweets factory in Banten kills 3 and
  leaves 36 missing.
- **Also today** — the EU and Indonesia sign a trade deal after ten
  years of talks; the FBI crime database is breached; a Russian missile
  hits Kryvyi Rih; a magnitude 6.7 earthquake shakes Peru's Andes.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-drawn
`entry.png` (1024×1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
